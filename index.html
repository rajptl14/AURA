What you're actually seeing

An aura isn't one thing. It's seven.

Each layer sits a little further from the body and carries different information — from physical vitality near the skin to a sense of connection at the outer edge. Hover a layer to see where it sits.

Etheric
Closest to the body. Reflects physical vitality — sleep, tension, and how grounded you feel today.
Emotional
Carries what you're feeling, including what you haven't said out loud yet.
Mental
Your thought patterns — clarity, rumination, and the beliefs running quietly in the background.
Astral
The heart layer. How you connect — to people, to work, to the room you're standing in.
Etheric Template
A kind of blueprint layer — where intention and will start to take shape before action.
Celestial
Where spiritual feeling lives — awe, faith, the sense of something larger than the everyday.
Ketheric
The outermost field. Broad, quiet, and slow to change — a sense of your place in things.
